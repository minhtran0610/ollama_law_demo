COMPANIES ACT (OSAKEYHTIÖLAKI 624/2006) — CHAPTER 6 AMENDMENTS SUPPLEMENT
Covers amendments after 660/2022, for use alongside the English translation
current only to 660/2022.

Scope: Chapter 6 (yhtiön johto — company management) only. Compiled from
Finlex/Edilex changelog entries and secondary sources; NOT a substitute for
the primary statutory text. Confidence labels (CONFIRMED / INFERRED /
NOT CONFIRMED) indicate how directly each item was verified — see notes
under each entry.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ACT 1252/2023 (HE 20/2023 vp)
   Given: 21.12.2023 | Published: 29.12.2023 | In force: 31.12.2023
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS: CONFIRMED (via secondary source, not primary text)

Added Chapter 6, Sections 16a–16f (block of new provisions). Implements the
EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) in Finland, alongside
parallel amendments to the Accounting Act (kirjanpitolaki) and Auditing Act
(tilintarkastuslaki).

Section 16a: Makes the board of directors (hallitus) collectively
responsible for (i) financial reporting and audit, (ii) sustainability
reporting as required under the Accounting Act, and (iii) sustainability
assurance as required under the Auditing Act.

Sections 16b–16e: Not independently verified in this research pass —
presumed to contain related CSRD implementation detail (e.g. governance
body responsibilities, disclosure mechanics). Flagged for future
verification.

Section 16f ("Poikkeukset kestävyysraportointia koskevista velvollisuuksista"
— Exceptions to the obligations concerning sustainability reporting):
heading confirmed via Edilex; substantive content not independently
verified, but per the graduated CSRD rollout in effect at the time, likely
contained phase-in/exception rules keyed to company size and listing status
(e.g. public-interest entities >500 employees from FY2024, other large
companies from FY2025, smaller listed companies from FY2026). This
provision was repealed by 558/2026 (see below) once that graduated,
listing-sensitive structure was replaced by a flat threshold — INFERRED,
not textually confirmed.

Sources: Excellence Finland, "Hyvä hallinto ja vastuullisuus"
(www.excellencefinland.fi/hyva-hallinto-ja-vastuullisuus/); Edilex
Osakeyhtiölaki reference card (www.edilex.fi/lainsaadanto/20060624).

2. ACT 652/2024 (HE 60/2024 vp — "laeiksi osakeyhtiölain 6 luvun ja
   kauppakamarilain 2 §:n muuttamisesta")
   Given: 22.11.2024 | Published: 26.11.2024 | In force: 28.12.2024
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS: CONFIRMED

Added Chapter 6, Section 9a (plus an annex/liite). Implements an EU
board-gender-balance requirement for listed companies (pörssiyhtiöt).

Applicability note: NOT related to EEA residency requirements. Does NOT
apply to non-listed companies — a private, non-listed Oy (however owned)
falls outside this section's scope regardless of founder composition.

Source: Edilex Osakeyhtiölaki changelog (www.edilex.fi/smur/20060624).

3. ACT 558/2026 (HE 38/2026 vp — "laeiksi kirjanpitolain ja tilintarkastuslain
   1 luvun 1 §:n ja 3 luvun 5 a §:n muuttamisesta"; osakeyhtiölaki portion
   published separately as "Laki osakeyhtiölain muuttamisesta")
   Given: 16.6.2026 | Published: 23.6.2026 | In force: 30.6.2026
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS: CONFIRMED (context and substance) / INFERRED (exact 16a wording)

Note on sourcing: HE 38/2026's title names only the Accounting Act and
Auditing Act. The Companies Act, Cooperatives Act (osuuskuntalaki), and
Securities Markets Act (arvopaperimarkkinalaki) were amended as
consequential provisions under the same bill, to keep implementation of
the underlying EU directive coherent across all four acts. This is why an
act titled "Laki osakeyhtiölain muuttamisesta" cites HE 38/2026 as its
legislative basis despite the bill's title never mentioning osakeyhtiölaki.

Implements EU Directive (EU) 2026/470 (the "Omnibus I" sustainability-
reporting amending directive). Parliament approved the bill as amended on
3.6.2026; presidential confirmation 16.6.2026.

Chapter 6 changes:
  - Section 16f: REPEALED (kumottu).
  - Section 16a: AMENDED (muutettu). Exact before/after wording not
    independently verified — see note under 1252/2023 above. Most likely
    effect: updates the cross-reference so the board's collective
    responsibility for sustainability reporting tracks the Accounting
    Act's new, narrower definition of a "kestävyysraportointiyritys"
    (sustainability-reporting company), rather than changing WHO bears
    the responsibility. INFERRED, not confirmed against statutory text.

Substantive reform (Accounting Act, for context — not itself Chapter 6,
but explains why 16a/16f changed):
  - Mandatory sustainability reporting is now limited to companies/parent
    companies averaging (over the last two completed financial years)
    >1,000 employees AND >€450 million turnover.
  - Stock-exchange listing no longer triggers the obligation on its own.
  - The balance-sheet-total threshold is dropped from the criteria.
  - Result: the population of reporting-obligated Finnish companies is
    expected to fall to roughly one-tenth of the prior figure; many
    listed companies that began CSRD reporting in the FY2024 first wave
    fall out of scope.
  - Generally applies to financial years starting 1.7.2026 or later, with
    optional early application for FYs starting on/after 1.1.2026.
    Companies dropping below the new thresholds may still opt in
    voluntarily by board decision.

Applicability note: Confirms none of this reaches a private, non-listed,
sole-founder Oy — the sustainability-reporting thresholds (under either
the pre-2026 graduated version or the current flat threshold) only ever
applied to much larger or listed entities.

Sources: Hannes Snellman legal alert, "Kestävyysraportoinnin muutokset

(www.finlex.fi/en/government-proposals/2026/38).
